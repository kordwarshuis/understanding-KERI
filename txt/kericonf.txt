Frequently Asked Questions Registration & Tickets When does registration open? Registration for MINI KERICONF26 and KERICONF27 is not open yet. The registration opening dates are yet to be announced. Can I transfer my ticket to someone else? Yes, tickets are transferable up to 48 hours before the event. Contact us at ticketing@keri.foundation to process a transfer. Do you offer student discounts? Yes! Students receive 70% off with valid student ID. Email student@keri.foundation for the discount code. Do I receive complementary tickets as a speaker? In general, speakers receive one free ticket for themselves. If a speaker is also a sponsor (or has already paid for a ticket), they may receive additional tickets as part of the sponsorship. Otherwise, there are no additional complimentary tickets for speakers. Event Details What should I bring? Bring your conference badge (provided at registration), laptop for workshops, business cards for networking, and comfortable shoes. Is there a dress code? Business casual attire is recommended, but we want you to be comfortable. Tech industry standard applies. Will sessions be recorded? Yes, the sessions will be recorded. Recordings will be made available to sponsors, speakers, and registered attendees approximately one week after the conference. At a later stage, selected recordings may also be released as educational material for a broader audience. Please note that the conference is designed primarily as an in-person experience, so attending on-site is the best way to fully participate. Can I attend remotely? This year’s conference is in-person only. Recordings are available to ticket-holders after the conference. Food & Accommodations Are meals included? All ticket include breakfast and lunch on both days. Dinner is also included on day 1. When you buy a ticket, you will receive a name badge at the registration desk. Meals will only be provided for attendees with a KERICONF name badge. Do you accommodate dietary restrictions? Yes! Please indicate any dietary restrictions during registration and we will accommodate them. Are there hotel recommendations? Hotel recommendations and partner rates will be announced once the conference location has been finalized. See our hotel recommendations page for a list of partner hotels with special conference rates. Networking & Activities Are there networking opportunities? Yes! The KERICONF is a gathering of a currently still small community. We have dedicated networking sessions like the Meet&Greet with entertainment, and a joint dinner on Day 1. Can I exhibit or sponsor? Yes! Sponsorship and exhibition opportunities are available. Contact sponsors@keri.foundation for the prospectus. How do the 5-minute commercial talks work? The 5-minute commercial talks are short, high-impact presentations. Make sure to rehearse your talk so it comfortably fits within the time limit—aim for around 4 minutes and 30 seconds, as speakers will be strictly cut off at 5 minutes. Sponsors receive priority in the lineup. To participate, sign up by emailing speaker.input@keri.foundation, and be sure to confirm your participation with the organizing team during the dinner. Sponsor Questions How do I claim my free tickets as a sponsor? As a sponsor, you will receive a 100% discount code from the organization. To claim your tickets, go to the ticket sales page on SimpleTix and select the standard ticket to order. During checkout, you can enter the discount code, which will reduce the ticket price to $0. If you have not received your code, please contact us at info@keri.foundation Where is the sponsor zone and what does it include? The sponsor zone will be located in the same space as the plenary sessions, ensuring strong visibility and interaction with attendees. Each sponsor will receive a table with two chairs. Power outlets will be available. If you require a monitor for demonstrations, you will need to bring your own. A monitor can be installed at your own risk; however, please note that if it is left unattended and something happens, we cannot take responsibility. On-screen demonstrations should have no sound. What is the topic of the sponsor panel sessions? The exact topics and speakers will be confirmed closer to the event, approximately two weeks in advance. These panels will be brief, and speakers will likely receive only one or two questions. Are there any deadlines for on-site branding submissions (e.g., banners and conference leaflets)? Banners can be brought on-site. For leaflets, we do not formally approve the content, but we expect all submitted materials to be accurate, appropriate, representative of your organization, and in line with the conference’s professional standards. Is there a timeline for sponsor setup and activations? In general, sponsors will have approximately 30–60 minutes before the evening event to set up. Setup is expected to take place between 4:30–5:30 PM on the eve of the conference, and materials can remain in place over the whole conference. If you will only arrive on the first day of the conference, setup can take place between 8:00–8:30 AM. Technical Is WiFi available? Yes, complimentary high-speed WiFi is available throughout the venue. Network details will be provided in your conference materials. Are there charging stations? Yes, charging stations are located throughout the venue for your convenience. For European participants: bring your own adapters. SEDI Conference I filled in the combination form for the SEDI Conference and KERICONF. Am I automatically invited to SEDI? Is the SEDI conference at the same location? Yes, the SEDI conference is at the same location as the KERICONF. Is there a combined attendance fee? The SEDI conference is by invitation only. We facilitate attendance for those who have chosen to attend KERICONF. If you plan to attend both conferences, we can offer a 10% discount code for KERICONF. Rules and Regulations Why is the organisation so pushy about the input of presentations so early? To ensure a high-quality and well-coordinated conference, we ask speakers to submit their slides in advance. With many speakers to align, an early submission allows the organizing team to review presentations for overall quality, consistency, and fit within the program. This process also gives speakers the opportunity to receive feedback and make improvements before the event. Please note that submitted slides do not need to be final—minor adjustments can still be made later. What are the rules and regulations about licensing, privacy, and inclusion?
